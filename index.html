How to read this: wOBA is overall offensive value per plate appearance (like OBP but weighted so a homer counts more than a walk; league average is about .310). xwOBA is what that number should be based on how hard and at what angle the ball was hit, plus strikeouts and walks. When actual beats expected the player is running good luck; when expected beats actual, bad luck. The Luck column is actual minus expected, in points (thousandths). Δx is last-30-day expected production minus season expected production, in points: for hitters positive is improving, for pitchers positive means hitters are doing more damage.

Chips: surging/sharp = real improvement in the underlying quality. Fading = real decline. Over water = results better than the underlying quality supports, expect regression. Unlucky = results worse than deserved, expect a bounce. Velo down = fastball velocity off 0.6+ mph over 30 days, an early fatigue or injury flag.
